## Instagram

- Name: Presence
- Username: @presence.me
- Profile picture: upload `presence-profile.png`.
- Website link: https://getpresence.me
- Contact email: hello@getpresence.me
- Suggested bio:

  Thoughtful companionship. Creative opportunities.
  Book an hour or apply to model ↓

In Instagram, open your profile, select Edit profile, and add the picture, name, bio, and website under Links. Switch to a professional account if you want contact buttons and business tools; choose the category that best describes your business. Set up two-factor authentication and give the other founders access through Meta's account tools rather than sharing passwords.

## LinkedIn company page

- Website: https://getpresence.me
- Logo: upload `presence-profile.png`.
- Cover: upload `linkedin-company-cover.png`.
- Tagline: Thoughtful companionship. Creative opportunities.
